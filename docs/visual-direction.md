# PulseFlow visual direction

Reference: https://www.fossclub.tech/ (inspected October 29, 2026).

Adapt its bold grotesque headings, paper-cut lettering, handwritten annotations, mint accents, ink outlines, and offset paper shadows to PulseFlow's requested light theme. Keep the app focused on assignment, discussion, and independent review; decorative collage stays mostly on the public landing page.

The homepage uses an explicitly labelled example workspace rather than claiming live team activity. The app retains its actual data, project boundaries, readable status badges, and simple navigation. Tape is decorative, anchored to the centre of its own card, and does not cover controls. Mobile layouts stack the hero and preview without horizontal overflow. Reduced-motion preferences disable marquee and reveal animation.

Fonts are self-hosted Google Fonts Bricolage Grotesque and Caveat Latin WOFF2 subsets with font-display: swap; their SIL Open Font License files are in public/fonts. PulseFlow uses its own branding and product content; no FOSS Club member photos or club identity assets are included.
